Rwandan Brochettes

Charcoal-grilled goat skewers served with fried plantain, ubugali and fiery akabanga chilli oil.

  • Cook40 min
  • Prep1 hr marinating
  • Serves4
  • LevelEasy
Start cooking
What you need

Ingredients

Change the servings and every quantity updates automatically. Original recipe serves 4.

For the brochettes

  • 800 g goat meat (or beef), cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 1 medium onion, cut into chunks
  • 1 green pepper, cut into chunks
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 8 skewers (soak wooden skewers in water first)

To serve

  • 4 ripe plantains, peeled and sliced
  • Oil for frying
  • Akabanga chilli oil, a few drops to taste
The story behind the dish

Culture on a plate

Brochettes — skewers of grilled meat — are one of the most popular meals in Rwanda. Goat brochettes (brochettes z'ihene) are the classic, cooked over charcoal in bars and restaurants across Kigali, especially in the evenings and at weekends with friends.

They are traditionally served with fried plantains or potatoes, a fresh tomato and onion salad, and akabanga — a very hot Rwandan chilli oil sold in tiny yellow bottles. Just one or two drops is enough!

Method

Cooking instructions

Marinate the meat

Mix the garlic, ginger, oil, paprika, black pepper, salt and lemon juice. Coat the meat and leave to marinate for at least 1 hour.

Thread the skewers

Thread the meat onto the skewers, alternating with pieces of onion and green pepper.

Grill over charcoal

Grill over hot charcoal for 12–15 minutes, turning every few minutes, until the meat is browned outside and cooked through.

Fry the plantains

While the meat grills, fry the plantain slices in hot oil for 2–3 minutes per side until golden. Drain on paper.

Serve

Serve the brochettes hot with the plantains, a simple tomato and onion salad and a few drops of akabanga.

Cook's tip: Goat meat can be tough — a lemon marinade and cutting the meat into even cubes help it cook evenly and stay tender.
