Roasted Sweet Potatoes

Whole sweet potatoes roasted until soft, sweet and caramelised, a simple, filling staple across the Great Lakes.

  • Cook50 min
  • Prep5 min
  • Serves4
  • LevelEasy
Start cooking
What you need

Ingredients

Change the servings and every quantity updates automatically. Original recipe serves 4.

  • 4 medium sweet potatoes, scrubbed
  • 2 tsp vegetable oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Avocado or beans, to serve (optional)
The story behind the dish

Culture on a plate

Sweet potatoes are one of the most important crops in Uganda and Rwanda, where they are called ibijumba in Kinyarwanda. They grow well on small family farms, and both the roots and the leaves are eaten.

Families often roast them whole in the hot ashes of a cooking fire and enjoy them for breakfast with tea or milk, or as a side with beans and avocado. Orange-fleshed varieties are promoted in the region because they are rich in vitamin A.

Discover more dishes from this region on our East Africa page.

Method

Cooking instructions

Wash the potatoes

Scrub the sweet potatoes well under running water. Leave the skins on, they protect the flesh while roasting.

Prepare to roast

Heat the oven to 200 °C. Rub the potatoes with the oil and salt and place them on a baking tray. (Traditionally, bury them in the hot ashes of a charcoal or wood fire instead.)

Roast

Roast for 40–50 minutes, turning once, until a knife slides easily into the centre and the skins are slightly blistered.

Serve

Let them cool for a few minutes, peel or eat with the skin, and serve with tea, beans or avocado.

Cook's tip: Choose potatoes of a similar size so they all finish cooking at the same time.
