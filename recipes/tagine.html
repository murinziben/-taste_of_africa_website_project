Moroccan Tagine

Chicken braised slowly with saffron, ginger, preserved lemon and green olives in a cone-lidded clay pot.

  • Cook1 hr 35
  • Prep20 min
  • Serves4
  • LevelMedium
Start cooking
What you need

Ingredients

Change the servings and every quantity updates automatically. Original recipe serves 4.

  • 1½ kg chicken pieces (thighs and drumsticks)
  • 2 medium onions, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 pinch saffron threads, soaked in 2 tbsp warm water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 preserved lemon, rind only, sliced
  • 1 cup green olives
  • 1 cup water
  • A handful of fresh coriander and parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
The story behind the dish

Culture on a plate

The word tagine describes both the dish and the pot it is cooked in: a shallow clay base with a tall, cone-shaped lid. As the stew cooks over low heat, steam rises, cools in the lid and drips back down, so meat becomes meltingly tender with very little water. The technique is deeply rooted in Amazigh (Berber) cooking.

Moroccan tagines are known for layering flavours: warm spices like saffron, ginger and cinnamon, sour notes from salt-preserved lemons and the savoury bite of olives. Tagines are served in the centre of the table and eaten with bread, which is used to scoop up the sauce.

Method

Cooking instructions

Marinate the chicken

Mix the garlic, olive oil, ginger, turmeric, cumin, paprika, saffron water, salt and pepper. Rub over the chicken and leave for at least 30 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).

Build the base

Spread the grated onion over the base of the tagine or a heavy pot. Arrange the chicken on top, tuck in the cinnamon stick and pour in the water.

Cook slowly

Cover and cook over low heat (use a heat diffuser under a clay tagine) for 45–50 minutes, until the chicken is tender. Check now and then and add a splash of water if needed.

Add lemon and olives

Scatter the preserved lemon rind and olives over the chicken. Cover again and cook for 15 more minutes so the sauce thickens.

Finish and serve

Sprinkle with chopped coriander and parsley. Bring the tagine to the table and serve with crusty bread or couscous.

Cook's tip: Preserved lemons are lemons cured in salt for a month. Rinse the rind before using and discard the pulp, a little gives a lot of flavour.
